# Resolve accepted Turns to terminal values

Once `AgentSession.startTurn(input)` has returned a Turn Handle, its `result` promise always resolves to a JSON-safe `TurnResult` discriminated as `completed`, `failed`, or `interrupted`, exactly matching the Turn Handle's final Turn Event. A completed result contains the Turn ID, the final complete Assistant Message, and optional final usage; failed and interrupted results contain the Turn ID, their structured terminal cause, and optional final usage, but no event list, tool history, or Turn transcript. Turn Failures and interruptions are values rather than promise rejections, while a separate Command Rejection may still be thrown by any public operation that cannot accept its command; a Harness that reports completion without a final Assistant Message is treated as an Adapter protocol failure.
